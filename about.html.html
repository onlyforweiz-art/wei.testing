About the Chapter

Who we are and what we stand for.

The Junior Philippine Institute of Accountants (JPIA) — Sorsogon State University Local Chapter is the official student organization for BS Accountancy students, dedicated to academic excellence, professional development, and community service.

We serve as the bridge between the classroom and the profession — organizing seminars, review sessions, outreach programs, and fellowship activities that shape well-rounded future CPAs.
